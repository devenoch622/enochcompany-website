WHO WE ARE

About Enoch Tech

Enoch Tech Company is a technology-focused organization committed to building useful, secure, modern and accessible digital solutions. We combine software engineering, cybersecurity, IT knowledge, artificial intelligence and technology consulting to help people and organizations turn ideas into practical technology.

Technology With Purpose

Our philosophy is simple: technology should do more than exist. It should solve problems, improve productivity, create opportunities and make people's work easier.

We approach every project by first understanding the problem, the people who will use the solution and the goals the technology needs to achieve.

From websites and applications to cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and IT consulting, our focus is on creating solutions that are practical, reliable and prepared for future growth.

FOUNDER & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Enoch S. Kettor Sr.

Enoch S. Kettor Sr. is the founder and CEO of Enoch Tech Company. His vision is to build a technology organization that combines software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, IT solutions and digital innovation to solve real-world problems.

As a technology-focused leader, he emphasizes continuous learning, responsible cybersecurity, practical software development and creating opportunities through digital technology. The company's identity — We Build. We Secure. We Innovate. — reflects this leadership philosophy.

The CEO profile area also serves as a professional media center where company leadership photos, interviews, presentations and technology videos can be shared with visitors, partners and employees.
